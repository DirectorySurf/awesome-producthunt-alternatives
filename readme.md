## 🚀 Launch Platforms

* [BuildBoard](https://altern.ai/buildboard) – Launch anything.
* [Semi Launch](https://semilaunch.com/) – Weekly launchpad where indie makers submit almost anything, from apps to unusual projects, and get votes and visibility.
* [Peerlist Launchpad](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) – Weekly launches tied to your professional profile.
* [Uneed](https://uneed.best) – Daily launches and newsletter exposure for indie products.
* [Fazier](https://fazier.com/) – Daily launch feed with badges and backlinks.
* [MicroLaunch](https://microlaunch.net/) – Daily leaderboards and extended exposure for makers and startups.
* [Betapage](https://betapage.co/) – Daily startup listings.
* [PeerPush](https://peerpush.net/) – Product discovery platform for indie makers.
* [Nick Launches](https://nicklaunches.com/) – Showcase a new product, reach other builders, and earn a backlink.

## 🧪 Pre-Launch & Beta

* [BetaList](https://betalist.com/) – Launch your startup early and get early adopters.
* [Launching Next](https://www.launchingnext.com/) – Submit your startup for listing.
* [StartupBase](https://startupbase.io/) – Share your startup and get discovered.

## 💬 Communities

* [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/show) – Submit with “Show HN” for developer-focused visibility.
* [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/products) – Share your product with a community of indie founders.
* [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/) – Subreddits like r/SideProject, r/startups, and r/Entrepreneur.
* [DEV Community](https://dev.to/) – Especially good for developer tools.

## 📚 Software Directories

* [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/) – List your product as an alternative to existing tools.
* [SaaSHub](https://www.saashub.com/) – Software discovery and alternatives, useful for long-term visibility.
* [Side Projectors](https://www.sideprojectors.com/) – Showcase and even sell side projects.
* [Startup Stash](https://startupstash.com/) – Tool directory and startup resources.
* [Startup Tools List](https://startuptoolslist.com) – Curated directory of startup tools across business modeling, validation, content creation, growth, and fundraising.

## 🧐 Niche Directories

* **AI/ML Tools**

  * [Altern](https://altern.ai/) – AI tools directory offering free submissions, curated lists, and professional profiles for AI products.
  * [Futurepedia](https://www.futurepedia.io/) – Large directory of AI tools, updated daily.
  * [AI Directory](https://aidirectory.website/) – Free catalog of newly launched and standout AI tools.
  * [Submit AI Tools](https://submitaitools.org/) – Discover, compare, and list AI tools for free.
  * [Best of AI](https://bestofai.io/) – Directory of 1000+ handpicked AI tools, sorted by profession and category.
  * [Launch Llama](https://launchllama.co/) – AI tools directory and launch platform paired with a founder newsletter.

* **Developer Tools & Open Source**

  * [Dev Hunt](https://devhunt.org/) – Launchpad for developer tools with weekly competitions.
  * [Open Saas Directory](https://opensaas.directory) – Curated open-source SaaS projects and self-hosted alternatives.

* **macOS Apps**

  * [MacNative](https://macnative.io) – Hand-picked directory of the best macOS apps, with screenshots for every listing.
  * [Awesome Mac Launch Platforms](https://github.com/macnative/awesome-mac-launch-platforms) – Curated list of where to launch a macOS app: submission platforms, subreddits, GitHub awesome lists, and newsletters.

* **Productivity**

  * [Productivity Directory](https://productivity.directory/) – Curated directory of productivity apps and tools, with reviews and guides.

* **Crypto & Web3**

  * [Crypto Tools Directory](https://cryptotoolsdirectory.com/) – Share your crypto tools and products with a crypto community.
  * [LaunchCaster](https://launchcaster.xyz/) – Decentralized product launches.

* **Regional**

  * [Startuplist Africa](https://startuplist.africa/) – Focused on African startups.
